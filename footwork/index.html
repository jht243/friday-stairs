The Workout

Friday Stairs is a high-energy HIIT-based outdoor workout that alternates between stairs and bodyweight exercises lasting approximately 30 minutes.

The standard format consists of six four-minute blocks:

  • • RUN UP THE STAIRS
  • • COMPLETE A BODYWEIGHT MOVEMENT
  • • RUN BACK DOWN
  • • REPEAT.

It's challenging, but not exclusive. All fitness levels are welcomed.

Regular sign-ups drop every Monday at 12 PM EST on the @fridaystairs Instagram story.

Our Mission

Democratize health and wellness by building the world's most meaningful free weekly fitness ritual, turning public spaces into places of growth, connection, and collective momentum.

Friday Stairs exists to help people rise through movement, shared effort, and the simple act of showing up together every Friday morning.
