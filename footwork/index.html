Friday Stairs is a free weekly outdoor fitness ritual.

/ˈfrīˌdā ˈsterz/
n. 1. A free weekly HIIT-based outdoor workout built around stairs, bodyweight movement, music, intention, and collective energy.

2. The act of rising together — turning individual challenges into shared momentum, every Friday morning.

The why

Friday Stairs started because the founders saw a need that other fitness brands missed. People don't need yet another workout. They need a place to show up as they are, move through something challenging, and feel the quiet strength of being surrounded by others doing the same.

No gym. No membership. No performance. Just stairs, fresh air, and people choosing to climb together.

What began as a workout became a ritual. People gather after. They bond. Then they come back — not just for the sweat, but for the feeling of being part of something bigger than themselves.

Our mission is to democratize health and wellness by building the world's most meaningful free weekly fitness ritual — turning public spaces into places of growth, connection, and collective momentum. From Tampa to every city worldwide, Friday Stairs exists to help people rise through movement, shared effort, and the simple act of showing up together.

The format

Friday Stairs is a weekly, high-energy HIIT workout built around stairs, bodyweight movement, music, intention, and collective energy. The team arrives, music starts, and the group warms up together. Each session opens with a weekly intention — something simple and human to carry into the climb and beyond.

The workout lasts about 30 minutes — six four-minute blocks of running the stairs and moving at the top, rotating through lower body, upper body, full body, and finisher work, closing with a wall sit and core. The experience ends with a team photo, high fives, and time to connect.

It's challenging, but not exclusive. All fitness levels are welcomed.
