Friday Stairs is a free weekly fitness ritual.

/ˈfrīˌdā sterz/
n. 1. A 30-minute workout built around stairs, movement, music, intention, and collective energy.

2. A space for people to show up as they are, move through something challenging, and leave more connected than they arrived.

3. A reminder that everyone is climbing something, and nobody has to climb alone.

Our mission

Democratize health and wellness by building the world's most meaningful free weekly fitness ritual, turning public spaces into places of growth, connection, and collective momentum.

From Tampa to every city worldwide, Friday Stairs exists to help people rise through movement, shared effort, and the simple act of showing up together every Friday morning.

Friday Stairs curates an environment where group fitness transforms into a place that feels like home.

Our vision

Shape a world where movement, energy, and growth are shared and accessible experiences for all.

Friday Stairs is the platform for a generation choosing to rise together. If you're climbing, you belong.

The workout

Friday Stairs is a weekly high-energy HIIT-based outdoor workout that alternates between stairs and bodyweight exercises.

The workout lasts approximately 30 minutes. The experience ends with a team photo, high fives, and time to connect.

It's challenging, but not exclusive. All fitness levels are welcomed.

The Monday drop

The workout sign-ups drop every Monday at 12 PM EST on the @fridaystairs Instagram story.
