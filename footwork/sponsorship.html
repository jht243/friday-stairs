Why sponsor Friday Stairs.

Most brand activations interrupt people. Friday Stairs invites you into the moment they chose for themselves.

Authentic community.

A weekly ritual built on showing up, not selling in. Your brand earns trust by being part of the climb, not interrupting it.

Repeatable reach.

52 touchpoints a year. Real bodies, real sweat, real conversation. The same faces, and new ones, every week.

City as canvas.

Our gym is the city — stairs, plazas, parking decks. Sponsor content lives in vivid, photogenic urban backdrops.

Mission-aligned.

Health, wellness, accessibility, community. We democratize fitness; you reach people who value brands that do the same.

Content engine.

Every week is a shoot. Photos, reels, post-climb interviews. We deliver assets you can reuse across your own channels.

Real conversion.

Sampling, trial, code redemption, on-site activations. Engaged participants try things — and they tell their friends.
